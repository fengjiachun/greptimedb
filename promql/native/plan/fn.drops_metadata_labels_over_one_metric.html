Whether node drops PROMQL_METADATA_LABELS on its own over series whose labels are proved to tell apart and whose keys are not.

This is what the pair the proof leaves outside costs, stated rather than collected: the labels prove the drop (metadata_labels_keep_series_apart), nothing at or below it has collected already (canonical_below), and the scan’s primary key holds a column that can spell NULL in one series and '' in another. Those two decode to an absent and to an empty label, and the drop normalizes them into one - the case a collection used to be inserted for and that a name-dropping kernel over a metric-engine table, whose tag columns are all nullable, paid a merge for. The collection is gone (see metadata_labels_keep_series_apart) and this is the count that keeps the statements it would have covered observable.

Every node that drops the metadata labels on its own counts: a map of math, a histogram map, a parameterized map and an arithmetic or bool scalar broadcast, and the three that rewrite a sample’s own value - a name-dropping range function, timestamp(x) and a timestamp over an evaluated input.
